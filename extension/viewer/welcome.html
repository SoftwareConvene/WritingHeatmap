You're set

  • On a Doc, click the extension icon in Chrome's toolbar. A side panel opens beside the Doc with a short summary of each student.
  • Open the full view from that panel, or with the button in the Doc, to see the heatmap, play back the writing and print a report.
  • Click the icon on any other tab to open the class dashboard: paste a class's Doc links to see everyone at once.

Tip: pin the extension (the puzzle-piece icon → pin) so its icon is always in the toolbar. You can change these choices any time in Settings in the full view.
