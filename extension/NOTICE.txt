What it does with data: it reads a Google Doc's edit history, using your own
Google sign-in, only when you ask it to (from the document, or with "Analyze
all" on a class dashboard), and analyzes it inside your browser. Nothing is
sent to SoftwareConvene or anyone else. No document text is saved to disk.

It describes how a document was put together. It does not detect AI, does not
decide who wrote anything, and is not evidence of misconduct on its own.
