**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.31.zip` below, unzip it, and update the extension the same way as before.

**Shorter side panel**
- The color key is one short line per color, and the Summary, Who wrote what, Timeline, Dates and Notes boxes have shorter notes.
- The Passage box shows the category in one line, the passage, and only the measures that aren't zero. What else can produce the pattern, and every single edit, are under **More**.
- Edits name the student ("Student 3 typed…") instead of "Editor 2 (Student 3)".
- Timeline dates no longer print on top of each other.

**Editing time for each section**
- **Same section in every document** shows how long each student spent editing the checked section(s), e.g. "73 words · about 3 min editing", in Quick check (for each writer too) and in Full text.
- It's counted like "Time spent writing": the time between a student's edits in that section, leaving out pauses over 2 minutes. Reading, research and thinking with no typing aren't seen, and text written somewhere else and pasted in takes almost no time, so many words in very little time stands out.

**Short pastes show in red**
- A piece added all at once now counts from 15 characters (it was 30), so a short pasted phrase or term shows red instead of yellow. If a student's typing reaches the Doc in bigger bursts, the size still rises to fit their typing.
- A short paste inside a typed sentence is now colored on its own, without the typing around it.
- A template's starting text is no longer counted as a chunk in the student's timeline.
