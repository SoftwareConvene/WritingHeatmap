**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.13.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**New colours**
- **Red:** added in a large chunk (copy/paste, dictation or another tool). Red with stripes means it was then reworked: red/blue for some retyping, red/green for major revisions.
- **Yellow:** written straight through, with little sign of going back over it.
- **Blue:** some retyping.
- **Green:** major revisions.
- Each colour keeps its own icon and underline or outline, so the categories still differ on a black-and-white printout or for colour-blind readers.

**Watch it being written**
- **▶ Play whole document** (next to the colour buttons) plays the whole Doc being written, from the first keystroke.
- **▶ Play how this was written** at the top of the Passage box plays every edit behind the passage you clicked. Long passages now play in full.
- **▶ Play section** beside each heading plays everything under that heading, up to the next heading.
- New speeds: 64× and 256× play a long essay in under a minute. Playing starts as soon as the window opens.
