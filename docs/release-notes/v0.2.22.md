**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.22.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**Section boxes stay as you set them**
- Unticking the last ticked section no longer ticks the first one again. Untick the first section and tick the ones you want; with none ticked, the page asks you to tick one.

**Checkpoints load faster for the whole class**
- **See in every document** on the Checkpoints tab now works out that checkpoint first. Before, it waited until every checkpoint had been worked out for every student, which on a full class could take minutes and looked as if nothing had changed.
- The top of the page shows how many documents are ready ("Working this out: 12 of 25 ready").
- The same moment is never worked out twice, and a document that cannot be worked out shows "—" instead of waiting forever.
