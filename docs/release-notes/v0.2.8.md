**What a large insertion first said**
- Click a passage that arrived in a large chunk (a paste, dictation, another tool) and was changed afterwards. The **Passage** box now shows it **as first added** above how it reads **now**.
- Words removed since are struck through; words added since are underlined. A line under the two counts the words removed, added and kept.
- Words deleted from the edges of the chunk are shown too, but text that now sits in another passage is not pulled in.
- When a sentence is split into differently colored pieces, each piece shows the whole sentence's before and after.
- It shows up on the printed report for any passage you add to it.
- Typed text and chunks that were never changed show no comparison.
