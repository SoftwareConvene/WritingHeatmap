**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.5.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`. This version also asks for access to `docs.google.com/document` pages, which the class dashboard uses to read documents without opening them.

**Time**
- **Document as of:** a slider above the document. Drag it to see the document, with its colours, exactly as it stood at any moment. Tick marks show where writing sessions start and the due date.
- **When it was written:** a third colouring, *During school hours / Outside school hours / After the due date*. Set school hours in **Settings** (days and times) and a due date in **Dates for this document**.
- **Checkpoints** tab: pick dates (or add the due date) and see how many words each student had written in each section by then. **View** jumps the slider to that moment.

**Students**
- **Compare students** now shows when each student wrote (school vs. home vs. late), a **who wrote which section** grid, and **Print one page per student**.
- A large chunk that was then revised is **striped**: yellow/green when lightly revised, yellow/pink when heavily revised.

**Class dashboard** (button in the viewer's top bar)
- Paste a class's Doc links, one per line, optionally with a name in front. **Analyse all** reads them in the background; you don't open each file.
- **Class table:** sortable numbers for every student, a review mark, and **Download CSV**.
- **Same section in every document:** pick a section (a heading or a template prompt such as "Procedure:") and read that part of every student's copy, one after another, with its colours. **Open full document** jumps straight to that section.
- **Checkpoints** across the whole class, for the whole document or one section.

**Also**
- **Jump to section** now includes template prompts, not just headings.
- The check against Google's copy of the text is stricter, so a partly rebuilt paragraph is shown as *History unclear* instead of passing.
