**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.7.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**Sections are the Doc's headings**
- Checkpoints, **Jump to section**, the per-student pages and the class dashboard now use only the paragraphs styled **Heading 1–6**, the same ones a Google Docs table of contents lists. Template lines like "Hypothesis:" or "Question 2:" are no longer sections on their own, so the list stays short.
- The Title and Subtitle are not sections.
- If a student types on a heading's line from the template, that heading keeps its template name, so their copy still lines up with everyone else's.
- **Settings → "Sections are headings only"** is on by default. Turn it off to bring back the old list: the Title and template lines such as "Hypothesis:" count as sections too. The class dashboard picks up the change the next time you press **Analyze all**.
- No sections in the Doc? Checkpoints show whole-document totals. To split them up, style each section title as a heading (**Format → Paragraph styles**) in the template before copying it to students, or turn the setting off.

**Class dashboard**
- Click the extension icon on any tab that isn't a Google Doc (a new tab, Classroom, your email) and the class dashboard opens. On a Doc, the icon still opens the heatmap for that Doc.
- A dashboard's name and links now save as you type. Renaming one or pasting new links sticks without pressing **Analyze all**. Pick a saved dashboard from the list at the top; **New** starts another.
- The analyzes themselves are still not saved, so press **Analyze all** after reopening a dashboard.
