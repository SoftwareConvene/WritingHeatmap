**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.2.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

- **Template copies no longer count as student writing.** When a Doc's very first edit adds a block of text (as happens when Classroom or "Make a copy" creates it from a template), that text is treated as provided: grey, and left out of every bar and percentage. A banner says so, with a one-click **Count it as the student's instead**.
- Typing an answer in front of template text (for example before "Question 2: …") counts as writing, not revising.
- The summary bar now says plainly that it covers students' text only.
