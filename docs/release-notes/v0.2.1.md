**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.1.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

- **Compare students:** a new tab beside *Document*. For each student, on one shared scale:
  - **What they put in:** characters typed, characters that arrived in large chunks (80+ at once, such as a paste, dictation or another tool), and characters deleted.
  - **Their final text, by how it was written:** composed in place, lightly revised, heavily revised and large insertion, as percentages and word counts.
  - **Show their text** jumps to the document with only that student's text highlighted in their color.
- The comparison is included in the printed report when more than one student edited the document.
- The **Writing Heatmap** button on Google Docs is bigger.
