**Install or update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.0.zip` below (not "Source code"), unzip it, and either **Load unpacked** the folder or replace your old folder and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

- **Only students' work is measured.** Template and prompt text, and anything the teacher adds, is shown in gray and left out. Your own account is recognized as Teacher; mark any editor as Teacher, Provided or Student in **Who wrote what**.
- **Who wrote what:** each student's share of the final text, words, characters typed and deleted, how much of the provided text they removed, and active time.
- **Color by "Who wrote it"** to see each student's text in their own color. Click a name to show only that student's work.
- **The document's own formatting:** headings, bold, italic, underline, lists, and tables shown as tables.
- **Search** the document, and **Jump to section** from its headings.
- Faster and sturdier on long or heavily rewritten documents. If an analysis ever stalls, the page now says so and offers a Stop button instead of waiting forever.
- Ordinary repeated phrases are no longer mistaken for text copied from elsewhere in the document.
