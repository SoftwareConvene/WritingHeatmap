**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.24.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**"See in every document" on the Checkpoints tab**
- With **Count words in: Whole document** chosen, the button now shows every student's whole document as it stood at that checkpoint. Before, it fell back to showing the first section, which looked as if nothing had happened.
- With a section chosen under **Count words in**, it shows that section, as before.
- The page now moves to the view straight away and shows a loading panel ("Going back to Monday…") with how many documents are ready and a progress bar. Each document appears as soon as it is done.

**Whole document in the section view**
- **Sections to show** has a new **Whole document** box, to read every student's full document one after another. It and the sections exclude each other.
