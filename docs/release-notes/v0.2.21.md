**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.21.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**Students' names on the dashboard**
- Links pasted without a label are now named after the student who works in each Doc, as Google lists its editors, instead of "Student 1, Student 2…". The names show in the class table, **Same section in every document**, Checkpoints and the CSV.
- Teachers are left out: you, anyone marked **Teacher** in Settings, and anyone who edits most of the class's Docs (such as a co-teacher leaving notes).
- Two students in one Doc show as "Name & Name", the one who wrote more first.
- A Doc no student has written in yet takes the name from its Google Classroom title, which starts with the student's name ("Name - Assignment").
- A label you type yourself in the links box is always kept.
- Names are kept only while the dashboard is open. They are never saved and never go into a class pack.
