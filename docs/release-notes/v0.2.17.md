**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.17.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**Setup guide**
- The first time Writing Heatmap is installed, a short guide opens: **school hours** (on or off, and which days and times), **colors** (standard or color-blind friendly) and whether to show the **button in Docs**. Each choice saves straight away. It ends with three tips on where to find things.
- Until the guide is finished, the full view, side panel and class dashboard show a **Finish setting up** link.
- **Settings → Run the setup guide again** opens it any time.

**School hours are off by default**
- School hours now start off. Turn them on in the setup guide or in **Settings**. This update also switches them off once on computers that already had the extension, so the guide (or Settings) is where to turn them back on.

**Also**
- The Settings line for testing tools now says what they are for. They are only needed for checking how Google's history is read on test Docs, so most teachers can leave them off.
- A replay that reaches the end switches back to **▶ Play** straight away.
