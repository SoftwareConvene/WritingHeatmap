**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.4.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

- The toolbar above the document (**How it was written / Who wrote it**, sections, search) now stays at the top while you scroll, so you can switch the colouring anywhere in the document.
- **Next edit ↓** and **↑** step through every passage that was revised, arrived in a large chunk, or is mixed or unclear, in reading order. Plain in-place writing and grey provided or teacher text are skipped. Each step opens that passage's details. When you are showing one student's text, it steps through only theirs.
