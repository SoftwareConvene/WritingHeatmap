**Sections in Docs with a table of contents**
- In a Doc with a table of contents, the contents lines were being taken for the headings they list. Each section then held a single line, and the real heading's section was missing most of its text.
- Headings are now found by lining up the whole Doc against Google's own copy of it, paragraph by paragraph. A line that only repeats a heading's words (the table of contents, a checklist, a rubric) no longer counts as the heading, and each section runs from its heading to the next one.
- Open the Doc again in the full view, or press **Analyze** again on the dashboard, to see the corrected sections.
