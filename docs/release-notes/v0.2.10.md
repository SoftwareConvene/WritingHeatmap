**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.10.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**Sections when the template has no headings**
- In 0.2.7 sections became the Doc's headings only (Heading 1–6). A template whose section titles are bold or plain lines ("Hypothesis:") then had no sections at all, so the class dashboard only offered *Whole document*.
- Now a Doc with no headings uses its **template lines** as sections instead. **Jump to section** says so at the top of its list. Docs that do use headings still use only their headings.

**Class dashboard: the sections most documents share**
- **Same section in every document** and the class **Checkpoints** list the sections found in at least half of the documents. A wrong link, or a copy of a different template, no longer adds sections nobody else has, and doesn't stop the rest from lining up.
- The others are one click away: **Show N more found in fewer than … documents**.
- A document that doesn't have the chosen section still shows "This document has no such section."
