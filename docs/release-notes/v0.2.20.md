**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.20.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`. Then press **Analyze** again on the dashboard (or reopen the Doc) to see the changes.

**Short pastes are caught**
- Before, text counted as a large insertion only at 80 characters or more, so a pasted sentence or definition shorter than that looked typed. The cut-off now follows each Doc: Google usually stores typing a few characters at a time, so anything from 30 characters up that arrived in one piece is now shown as a large insertion. If a Doc's typing arrives in bigger pieces, the cut-off rises with it (never above 80). The passage details and the Compare page show the cut-off used.

**Typed beside a paste that was then deleted**
- A student who pastes text, rewrites it next to the paste and then deletes the paste leaves only typing behind, which on its own looks written in place.
- Passages typed while a paste with many of the same words was in the Doc, where that paste was later deleted, now get a **dotted red underline**. Click one to see the deleted paste, when it was pasted and deleted, and the words the two share highlighted.
- The side panel, Compare students and a new dashboard column (also in the CSV) show how many words each student typed this way.
- Deleting a paste is no longer counted as revising the sentence next to it.
