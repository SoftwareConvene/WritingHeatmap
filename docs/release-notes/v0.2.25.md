**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.25.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**Play stays on the part you chose**
- **▶ Play section** and **▶ Play how this was written** now show only the edits made in that section or passage. Before, they also played the edits made just before and after each one, wherever they were, so a student who moved between sections made the replay jump to other parts of the Doc. Edits made elsewhere in the meantime still happen in the background, so the text stays right, but the replay no longer shows them or scrolls to them.
- Text copied or moved into a section (such as a draft pasted into the final draft) now replays from the moment it arrived there, followed by the edits made to it afterwards. Before, it replayed the draft being typed in the other section. Playing the draft section itself still shows that typing.
- **▶ Play whole document** is unchanged.
