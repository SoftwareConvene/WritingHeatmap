**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.6.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**New look**
- **Logo:** Writing Heatmap now has its own mark, a typing cursor on a tile in heatmap colours. It shows in the Chrome toolbar, on the extensions page and in the viewer.
- **Button in the Doc:** the launcher in the bottom-left corner is now dark, with the logo and a thin strip of heatmap colours. Hover over it and the colours fill the button. It is in the same place as before and can still be turned off in **Settings**.
