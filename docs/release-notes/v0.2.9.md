**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.9.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`. This version also asks for access to `docs.google.com/presentation` (Google Slides) pages.

**Google Slides: first test**
- The **Writing Heatmap** button and the toolbar icon now also work on a Google Slides deck. They don't colour Slides yet: they open a **Google Slides: testing** page.
- That page checks whether the deck's edit history can be read with your sign-in, shows what Google sent back (its layout only, not the text), and has a **Save raw history for testing** button.
- Use it only on a practice deck you made yourself. The steps for that deck are in `docs/fixtures.md` under *Google Slides (being tested)*. The saved file is what the Slides view will be built from.
- Nothing changes for Google Docs.
