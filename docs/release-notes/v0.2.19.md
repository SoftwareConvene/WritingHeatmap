**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.19.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`. Then press **Analyze** again on the dashboard (or reopen the Doc) so the sections are found again.

**Headings with dashes, quotes and symbols are found**
- A heading such as "Step 1 — Pick a Topic" was missed when it held a long dash, curly quotes or another special character. The heading above it then ran on over every missed section, and the missed sections could not be chosen at all.
- Headings are now matched on their letters and numbers, so the punctuation and emoji in a heading no longer matter.

**A heading used twice is told apart**
- When the same heading appears in two places (for example "Conclusion" in the written report and again on the board), each one is now its own section, named with the heading it sits under: **Conclusion (Write-Up: Report)** and **Conclusion (Write-Up: Board)**. Before, only the first could be opened.
