**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.15.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`.

**Side panel: when each student wrote**
- Each student's card now has a **writing timeline**: one strip from the first edit to the due date, the same for every student so they line up. Yellow blocks are writing sessions (red when a session was mostly large chunks), red marks are large chunks, the dashed line is the due date, and dotted lines are checkpoints.
- Under it: how many days they wrote on, how much came in the 24 hours before it was due, and how much after the due date.

**Side panel: checkpoints**
- If the Doc has checkpoints (set in the full view's **Checkpoints** tab, or in a class dashboard that lists this Doc), each card shows the student's words by each one: *By Draft (Oct 7): 120 words → now 520 words*.
- The due date also comes from the full view or the class dashboard. Change either and the panel updates.
