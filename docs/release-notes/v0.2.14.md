**Update:** download `writing-heatmap-extension-v0.2.14.zip` below, replace your old folder, and press the reload arrow on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`. This version also asks Chrome for a side panel.

**Side panel beside the Doc**
- On a Google Doc, click the **extension icon** in the toolbar. A panel opens beside the Doc with one short card per student:
  - how much of their text was **added in large chunks (copy/paste)**, **written straight through**, **retyped** or **revised**, as a bar and in words;
  - how much they typed, how many large chunks they added, and how much they deleted;
  - how long they were active, over how many sessions, and when they last edited;
  - how much was written at school, at home or after the due date.
- **See their writing** opens the full view showing only that student. **Open the full view** shows everyone.
- The panel follows whichever Doc tab you are on. Switch tabs and it summarizes that Doc.
- The **Writing Heatmap** button inside the Doc still opens the full view.

**Color-blind friendly colors**
- **Settings → Color-blind friendly colors** swaps red, yellow, blue and green for orange, blue, teal and purple, which stay distinct for the common kinds of color blindness. It applies everywhere: the full view, the side panel, the class dashboard, printouts and the button in the Doc.
